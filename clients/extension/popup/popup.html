Privacy & feedback

Usage statistics

The extension keeps one random resettable installation identifier, days since first use, visible sessions, and whether it has connected. If enabled after you continue, those values may be placed in the uninstall survey URL. Torrent names, hashes, paths, peers, trackers, credentials, logs, and errors are never included.

The uninstall URL is stored locally by Chrome. Opening it can disclose included values to browser history, website logs and referrers, the survey form, and the page’s Cloudflare analytics.
